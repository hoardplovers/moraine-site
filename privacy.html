Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 August 2026

Moraine collects nothing. There is no Moraine account, no server of ours, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. Your entries are stored on your devices and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud database. The developer cannot read them.

This page is deliberately specific rather than reassuring. Below is every piece of data the app touches, and every occasion on which anything leaves your device.

What the developer receives

Nothing. No entries, no metadata, no usage statistics, no crash reports tied to you, no email address. There is no mechanism in the app that sends anything to the developer, because there is no server to send it to.

If Apple sends the developer anything, it is Apple's own aggregate App Store reporting — download and sales counts, and optional anonymised crash and usage statistics if you have opted into sharing them with developers in your device settings. None of it identifies you, and none of it contains anything you wrote.

What stays on your device

  • Everything you capture: text, journal entries, links, photos, videos, sketches, and voice recordings with their transcripts.
  • The titles, summaries and tags generated for you, and — if you switch it on — the feeling an entry reads as. These are produced on the device by Apple Intelligence; the content of your entries is not sent anywhere to produce them.
  • Voice transcription, which runs on-device using Apple's Speech framework.
  • The semantic index that makes "search by meaning" work. It is built on-device and stored with your library.
  • Capture context you have switched on: coordinates, a place name, a weather snapshot, the device name.
  • Your settings, and the date your trial started (kept in the device keychain).

iCloud sync

If you are signed into iCloud, Moraine syncs your library through your own iCloud account, in what Apple calls the private database. It is your storage, under your Apple Account, subject to Apple's privacy policy. The developer has no access to it and no ability to grant anyone else access to it.

If you are not signed in, or you turn iCloud off for Moraine, everything stays on that one device and nothing syncs.

The things that do leave your device

There are four, and two of them are off unless you turn them on.

When What is sent, and to whom
You save a link Moraine fetches that page so it can keep the readable article text. The request goes to the website you saved — so that site sees a request from your IP address, as it would if you opened the link in a browser. Nothing about you or your library is included.
"Look up films, books and music"
off by default		 When a capture names a specific work, the title alone is sent to Apple's iTunes Search API (itunes.apple.com) or Wikipedia (en.wikipedia.org) to fetch a link and cover. The title is spotted on-device first; the text of your capture is never sent. This is the only feature that tells a server anything about what you wrote, which is why it is off until you ask for it.
"Save location with captures"
off by default		 Your coordinates are turned into a place name using Apple's MapKit, which means the coordinates go to Apple for that lookup. The place name and coordinates are then stored in your library.
"Save weather with captures"
off by default, and needs location on		 Your location is sent to Apple's WeatherKit to fetch current conditions. Apple states that WeatherKit does not associate requests with an identity.

Apple may also download on-device model assets — for speech transcription and for the semantic index — the first time they are needed. That is a download from Apple to you, not an upload.

Permissions, and what each is for

PermissionWhy
PhotosSo you can attach pictures and video. Moraine copies the ones you pick into its own library; it does not read the rest.
MicrophoneVoice notes. Recordings stay in your library.
Speech recognitionTranscribing those recordings, on-device.

CalendarOptional. Moraine reads your events to show them beside what you captured that day, and can add an event when you tap to accept a suggestion. Your events are not copied into Moraine's library or sent anywhere.
NotificationsOptional reminders. The text of a notification never contains anything you wrote — it says only that there is something to look at.
Face ID / Touch IDOptional lock over the app. Authentication is handled by the system; Moraine only learns whether it succeeded.

Spotlight

Entries are indexed into your device's Spotlight so they appear in system search. That index is local to your device and managed by the operating system. Removing an entry from Moraine removes it from the index.

Purchases

The purchase is handled entirely by Apple. The developer never sees your payment details, and receives no personal information from the transaction. The app records locally that it has been unlocked, and keeps the date your trial began in the device keychain.

Children

Moraine is a personal notebook and is not directed at children. It collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

Your data is yours to take

Settings → Backup → Export everything writes a folder of plain JSON with your photos and recordings beside it as ordinary files, readable without Moraine. It works during the trial, after the trial, and whether or not you have bought the app. Deleting the app deletes its local library; if you sync, the copy in your iCloud account is removed when you delete Moraine's data from iCloud in your device settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes and the previous versions remain in the site's public history. A change that meant Moraine started collecting something would be announced in the app before it took effect.
