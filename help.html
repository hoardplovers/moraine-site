Help

Short answers first. If something here doesn't cover it, email me — a real person reads it.

Getting things in

The + button covers a note, a journal entry, a link and a voice memo. Beyond that:

  • Share sheet — share anything from another app to Moraine.
  • Safari on Mac — the Moraine button clips the readable article. Enable it once in Safari → Settings → Extensions.
  • Siri — "Add a note to Moraine saying pick up oat milk", or "Capture in Moraine" for a two-step version.
  • Action Button / widget / Mac menu bar — one tap to a blank note or a recording.

Markdown works in the editor: **bold**, # headings, - lists.

Photos, and photo dates

Attach photos to any entry. If you add a photo that was taken on a different day, Moraine offers to date the entry when the photo was taken instead of today — useful when you are writing up a trip afterwards.

Finding things again

Search matches words and meaning. If you can't remember the words you used, describe the thing: "the argument about moving house" will find the entry even if none of those words appear in it. Meaning-based search is part of the purchase; keyword search always works.

Other ways in: Tags (a cloud or a ranked list), Map (where you captured things, if location is on), Calendar (what you wrote on a day, beside your diary), Photos (every picture as a wall), and Stats.

Looking back

Occasionally an old entry surfaces at the top of the library — either an anniversary, or something you wrote years ago that is close in meaning to what you have been writing lately. Dismissing it hides it for the day.

Journals

Journals keep separate things separate — Work, Travel, whatever you like. Make them in Settings → Journals.

  • Every entry starts in the default journal, called Journal. It can't be renamed or deleted.
  • The journal picker in the toolbar scopes the whole app — library, search, map, calendar, stats and photos all follow it.
  • Move an entry with a long press → Move to.
  • Deleting a journal never deletes its entries. They move back to the default journal.
  • Captures from outside the app — share sheet, Siri, widget, Safari — can't know which journal you were looking at, so they use the one you pick under "Captures from outside".

Coming from Day One

  1. In Day One, export the journal as JSON.
  2. Unzip it — in Files, long-press the zip and choose Uncompress.
  3. Moraine → Settings → Import from Day One → choose the folder.

Entries arrive with their dates, tags, places, weather, favourites, photos, video and one voice memo each — an entry that held two keeps the first. If the export is named after a journal, Moraine offers to keep it as its own journal. Importing the same export twice is harmless — entries already there are skipped. Nothing is dropped quietly: if something can't come across, the result says so and counts it.

Coming from Apple Journal

  1. In Journal, tap ⋯ then Export Entries.
  2. Unzip it — in Files, long-press the zip and choose Uncompress.
  3. Moraine → Settings → Import from Apple Journal → choose the folder.

Entries arrive with their dates, titles, writing, places, photos and video, and Moraine offers to keep them as their own journal. Journal entries are often a place and some pictures rather than writing, so a photo taken years before the entry keeps its own date and the entry keeps the day it was written.

Journal fills its export with things that are not photographs — maps, workout rings, contact cards. Those become the entry's place or a line of context instead of a library full of screenshots. Entries holding nothing but a map pin are left behind, and photos Journal itself could not export are counted: the result says how many of each rather than dropping them quietly.

Import is free and untimed. A large import then spends a while giving each entry a title, tags and its place in search — that part runs on your device, shows its progress, and can be paused.

Backups and export

Settings → Backup → Export everything writes a folder of plain JSON with your photos and recordings beside it as ordinary files. You can read it without Moraine, and keep it anywhere.

Export always works — during the trial, after it, bought or not. Restoring adds back anything missing and leaves what you already have alone, so restoring the same export twice is harmless.

Do this before you change devices, and before any big import. Moraine has no copy of your library, so nobody can recover it for you.

Sync

Moraine syncs through your own iCloud account. Settings shows the current state at the top.

  • "Not syncing — signed out of iCloud": sign in, and sync starts on the next launch. Nothing is lost in the meantime; it stays on that device.
  • New device shows nothing yet: give it a few minutes on Wi-Fi with the app open. Large libraries take a while for the first pass.
  • Two devices disagree: check both are signed into the same Apple Account and have iCloud Drive on.

The trial, and buying

Everything works for 14 days. After that: capture, browsing, keyword search, import and export keep working. Generated titles, summaries and tags, search by meaning, related entries and resurfacing are what the purchase turns back on.

Anything captured while the app is locked isn't lost or half-processed — it waits, and is picked up the moment you buy.

One purchase covers iPhone, iPad and Mac on the same Apple Account, and Family Sharing is on. On a new device: Settings → Restore purchase.

Troubleshooting

Titles and tags aren't appearing

Check Settings → Apple Intelligence. It needs a device that supports it, with it enabled in system settings. Without it, Moraine still titles and tags entries using a simpler on-device method. If the trial has ended, this is the part that unlocks with the purchase.

Voice notes aren't being transcribed

Settings → On-device models shows whether the speech model is installed. Tap Download models now if it isn't.

Search by meaning misses things

Entries need to have been indexed. After a large import that takes a while — the banner at the top of the library shows the progress. Settings → On-device models shows how much of the library is indexed.

No weather on entries

Weather needs location switched on as well, and Apple's weather service can take around half an hour to start answering for a newly installed build.
